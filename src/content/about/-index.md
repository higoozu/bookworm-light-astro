I never planned to become a traveler. It started as a weekend escape and slowly turned into a way of life. I fell in love with early mornings on empty streets, the way strangers become guides, and the quiet lessons you only hear when you stay long enough.

These days I travel with a small camera, a worn journal, and a promise to move gently through each place. I care about the details: local rituals, neighborhood cafes, and the stories that live behind the postcard view. This site is where I keep those memories—honest, imperfect, and deeply human.
